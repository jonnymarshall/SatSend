# v1.5.19 — Draft or sent: manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.19/draft-or-sent` · **Package version:** `1.5.19` · **Migration:** `0035`

An invoice is a draft or it's sent. There's no "Publish only"; every way to send
records the day it was sent, and that's the date shown everywhere.

## Quick smoke test

Run **TEST 1**, **TEST 2** and **TEST 4**.

## TEST 1: The Send menu

**Proves:** three ways to send, nothing called Publish.

1. **Run:** `npm run dev`, open **New Invoice**, fill it in with a fresh testnet
   address, then click the amber **Send** button.
2. **Expect:** three options: *Send now via email*, *Download PDF and mark as
   sent*, *Copy link and mark as sent* (with "Share the link yourself" under it).
   No "Publish" or "Publish only" anywhere: editor, invoice page, list row menu.

## TEST 2: Copy link and mark as sent

**Proves:** the link is copied, with a small confirmation.

1. **Run:** choose **Copy link and mark as sent**.
2. **Expect:** you land on the invoice. A small dark pill saying "✓ Link copied"
   appears at the bottom of the screen and fades after a couple of seconds, with
   nothing to close. Paste somewhere: it's `http://localhost:3000/invoice/<id>`,
   and opening it shows the client's page.
3. **Also try:** Safari, if you have it.

## TEST 3: Download PDF and mark as sent

1. **Run:** on another draft, choose **Download PDF and mark as sent**.
2. **Expect:** the PDF downloads; it says **Invoice date: <today>** (not "Date
   Created"); its file name ends in today's date.

## TEST 4: Dates

**Proves:** the date is the day it was sent; drafts have none.

1. **Run:** open **Invoices**.
2. **Expect:** drafts show *Not sent* under Date Sent; sent invoices show the day
   they were sent. Sorting by Date Sent puts the drafts together.
3. **Run:** open a draft's page. **Expect:** no Date Sent line, only Date Due.
4. **Run:** open a sent invoice's page, then its client page (View public
   invoice). **Expect:** "Date Sent: <day>" on yours; "Invoice date: <same day>"
   on the client's.
5. **DB check (SatSend-dev):**
   `select count(*) from invoices where status <> 'draft' and sent_at is null;` returns `0`.

## TEST 5: An email that doesn't go out

**Proves:** a failed email doesn't half-send an invoice.

1. **Run:** on a draft with a client email, choose **Send now via email** while
   email can't go out (for example with `RESEND_API_KEY` commented out in
   `.env.local` and the dev server restarted).
2. **Expect:** you land on the invoice, still a **Draft**, with a red note:
   "Email isn't set up here … Nothing was sent, so it's still a draft." Restore
   the key afterwards.

## TEST 6: The first sent date stays

1. **Run:** take an invoice sent with **Copy link and mark as sent**, then later
   **Send now via email** from its page.
2. **Expect:** its Date Sent doesn't change; the page says "Marked as sent on
   <first day>".

## TEST 7: Production (after merge)

1. **Run:** after migration 0035 is applied:
   `select count(*) from invoices where status <> 'draft' and sent_at is null;`
2. **Expect:** `0`. On satsend.me, the Send menu matches TEST 1.
