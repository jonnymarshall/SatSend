**Branch:** `v1.5/redesign` · **Package version:** `1.5.1`

This release changes how things look, not what they do. Each test is "open this,
check it looks right, and check the buttons still work". Start the dev server
(`npm run dev`) and sign in as usual. Use an invoice on the **test** database.

## TEST 1: Overall look + login

1. Sign out, then open `/login`.
2. **Expect:** a warm off-white page, the "SatSend.me" logo above a white card,
   a heading in the rounder heading font, and an amber "Send magic link" button
   with dark text.
3. Click into the email box. **Expect:** the border turns gold with a soft gold
   glow. It must **not** flash red first.
4. Send the magic link and sign in. **Expect:** "Check your email" shows in the
   same card style.

## TEST 2: Invoice list (`/invoices`)

1. **Expect:** a white header bar with the logo on the left and your email plus
   "Log out" on the right. Clicking the logo returns to the list.
2. **Expect:** status badges are a coloured dot plus a word: Draft grey, Pending
   blue, Payment detected violet, Paid green, Underpaid orange, Overdue red,
   Archived grey outline.
3. Open a row's "…" menu. **Expect:** a white menu with a soft shadow; "Delete"
   is in red text. Try one harmless action (e.g. Copy public link).
4. Tick a row, open **Bulk actions → Delete**, then **Cancel**. **Expect:** a
   white dialog on a dimmed, blurred page; Delete is a soft red button.
5. Click a column header to sort, use the filter box, and page with
   Previous/Next. **Expect:** all still work.

## TEST 3: New / edit invoice form

1. Click **New Invoice**. **Expect:** the form is split into white cards: invoice
   number, You/Client, line items + tax + due date, payment (BTC address and
   access code), then a totals card on the right.
2. Add two line items, drag one to reorder, remove one. **Expect:** all work.
3. Tick **No due date**. **Expect:** an amber tick box; the date picker hides.
   Untick it and pick a date. **Expect:** the calendar has the selected day in
   amber.
4. Leave the BTC address blank and try **Publish**. **Expect:** the error shows in
   red-ish text under the field (readable, not glaring).
5. **Save draft**, then edit it. **Expect:** the edit page looks the same.

## TEST 4: Invoice detail page

This is the one screen I could not preview without your login, so please look
closely.

1. Open an invoice from the list. **Expect:** "← Invoices" back link, a large
   invoice number, dates, and the status badge on the right.
2. **Expect:** white cards for From/Bill To, Share with client (for published
   invoices), Line items with totals underneath, and Bitcoin address/transaction.
3. **Expect:** the action buttons are white with dark text; Delete is soft red.
   "Edit draft", "View public invoice" and "Download PDF" still work.
4. Use **Publish/Send** and **Mark as** menus on a test invoice. **Expect:** the
   success or error notice shows as a green or red box.
5. **Expect:** the Activity card lists events with coloured icons.

## TEST 5: Public payer page (`/invoice/<id>`)

1. Open a published invoice's public link in a private window.
2. **Expect:** the logo top-left, a header card with the invoice number, dates,
   status and "Download PDF", then From/To, line items with totals, and "Pay with
   Bitcoin" in that order (the same order as before).
3. Click **Pay now in Bitcoin**. **Expect:** the QR code in a white frame, the BTC
   amount and address each with a "Copy" button that works.
4. Click **Mark as Payment Sent**, then **Cancel**. **Expect:** a white dialog with
   an amber progress bar.

## TEST 6: Access-code screen

1. Open the public link of an invoice that has an access code, in a private
   window.
2. **Expect:** the logo above a white card, and an amber Continue button.
3. Enter a wrong code. **Expect:** the box turns red and a red message shows.
4. Enter the right code. **Expect:** the invoice opens.

## TEST 7: Browser tab icon

1. Look at the browser tab. **Expect:** a dark rounded square with a white "S" and
   an amber dot (not the old icon). Hard-refresh if the old one is cached.
2. Optional, on an iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. **Expect:** the same mark
   as a full square icon.

## TEST 8: Phone width

1. On your phone (or browser dev tools at 390px wide), visit the list, form,
   detail, and payer pages.
2. **Expect:** no page scrolls sideways. On the list, the table scrolls sideways
   **inside its box** (by design for now; stacked rows are a later decision).
3. **Expect:** buttons and menu items are easy to tap with a thumb.

## TEST 9: Error and 404 screens

1. Visit `/nothing-here`. **Expect:** the small dark "S" icon, "Page not found",
   and a "Go home" button.

## TEST 10: PDF colours

1. Download any invoice PDF. **Expect:** the same layout as before, but the red
   accents are now a dark amber/gold. (The full PDF restyle is v1.5.1-H.)
