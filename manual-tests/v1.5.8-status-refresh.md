# v1.5.8 — Statuses update without opening the invoice: manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.8/status-refresh` · **Package version:** `1.5.8`

Before you start: stop your usual dev server and run **`npm run dev:sweep`**
instead of `npm run dev`. It prints
`[dev:sweep] background payment checks every 60s (test database)` at the top.
(If it refuses to start, it is because the app isn't pointing at the test
database; that's the safety check working.)

## TEST 1: The list moves on by itself (local)

1. Publish a testnet invoice and copy its payment details.
2. Go to `/invoices` and leave it open. Do **not** open the invoice.
3. Pay it from your testnet wallet (exact amount).
4. **Expect:** within about a minute the row changes to "Payment detected", and
   the payment-detected email arrives.
5. Keep `/invoices` open until the payment has 2 confirmations (testnet4 blocks
   are usually ~10 minutes each).
6. **Expect:** within about a minute of that, the row changes to "Paid" and the
   confirmation email arrives, still without opening the invoice.

## TEST 2: Nobody watching (local)

1. Publish and pay another testnet invoice, then close every SatSend tab
   (keep `npm run dev:sweep` running).
2. After it has 2 confirmations plus a couple of minutes, open `/invoices`.
3. **Expect:** it already says "Paid" and the email has already arrived.

## TEST 3: An invoice paid long after it was sent

1. Pick an unpaid testnet invoice published more than an hour ago (before this
   release its background watch had stopped).
2. Pay it and close the tabs.
3. **Expect:** it reaches "Paid" by itself after confirmation (the sweep puts
   old invoices back on the schedule, then checks every 30 minutes before a
   payment is seen, and every 10 minutes after).

## TEST 4: Live site (after merging and setting up cron-job.org)

1. Repeat TEST 2 on `https://satsend.me`.
2. **Expect:** "Paid" and the confirmation email within a few minutes of the
   second confirmation, with nobody on the site.
3. In cron-job.org, the job's history shows a run every minute with status 200.
