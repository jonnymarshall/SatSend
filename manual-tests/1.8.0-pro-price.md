# Release 1.8.0 (R-65) — Pro price: $15 a month, 10% off in bitcoin

**Branch:** `r-65/pro-price` · **Package version:** `1.8.0` · **Migration:** none

Pro is now $15 a month or $150 a year (2 months free). In bitcoin it's 10%
less: $13.50 a month or $135 a year.

## Setup (once)

1. `git checkout r-65/pro-price`
2. `npm run dev`

## Quick smoke test

TEST 1 and TEST 2.

## TEST 1 — Pricing page, monthly

**Run:** open http://localhost:3000/pricing

**Expect:**
- Pro shows **$15 / month**, with **$13.50 a month in bitcoin (10% off)** under it.
- Under the cards: "Pay for Pro by card, or in bitcoin for 10% off."

## TEST 2 — Pricing page, yearly

**Run:** click **Yearly**.

**Expect:** Pro shows **$12.50 / month**, with **Billed $150 yearly, or $135 in bitcoin** under it. The cards don't jump.

## TEST 3 — The FAQ

**Run:** open http://localhost:3000/faq, then **How much does Pro cost?** and **How do I pay for Pro?** (both under Pricing).

**Expect:**
- "$15 a month, or $150 a year (2 months free). Paid in bitcoin, it's 10% less: $13.50 a month or $135 a year."
- "In bitcoin, from any wallet (10% off), or by credit or debit card."

## TEST 4 — Homepage

**Run:** open http://localhost:3000 and scroll to the plans.

**Expect:** the same prices as TEST 1 and TEST 2.

## TEST 5 — Phone width

**Run:** TEST 1 and TEST 2 in a narrow window (or the browser's phone view).

**Expect:** the bitcoin line fits under the price and nothing overflows.
