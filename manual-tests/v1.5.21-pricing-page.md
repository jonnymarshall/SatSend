# v1.5.21 — Pricing page: manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.21/pricing-page` · **Package version:** `1.5.21` · **Migration:** none

## Quick smoke test

Run **TEST 1** and **TEST 2**.

## TEST 1: The page is real

1. **Run:** `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:3000/`, click **Pricing** in the menu.
   **Status check:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" http://localhost:3000/pricing` prints `200`.
2. **Expect:** headline "Simple pricing. No cut of your payments."; the two cards;
   "Compare plans" table; "Pricing questions" (5); the dark closing band. No
   "Coming soon" page.

## TEST 2: Monthly / Yearly toggle

1. **Run:** click **Yearly** (with the green "2 months free" tag).
2. **Expect:** Pro changes from **$10** to **$8.33 / month** with "Billed $100
   yearly" under it; Free stays $0; the cards don't jump. Click **Monthly**: back
   to $10. The same toggle works on the homepage's pricing section.

## TEST 3: The FAQ says what was agreed

1. **Run:** open each of the five questions.
2. **Expect:** bitcoin or credit/debit card; card renews until you turn it off
   (any time), bitcoin doesn't; you keep all your invoices; refund within 15 days
   ("contact support" links to `/contact`); Free is free forever.

## TEST 4: Phone width

1. **Run:** 390 × 844.
2. **Expect:** no sideways scrolling; the cards stack; the comparison table fits,
   with long entries wrapping.

## TEST 5: Production (after merge)

1. **Run:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" https://satsend.me/pricing`; open it.
2. **Expect:** `200` and the page from TEST 1; `curl -s https://satsend.me/pricing | grep -c 'name="robots"'` prints `0`.
