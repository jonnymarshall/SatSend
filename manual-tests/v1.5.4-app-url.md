# v1.5.4 — Production links no longer point at localhost: manual tests

**Branch:** `fix/app-url` · **Package version:** `1.5.4`

These checks are on the **live site** after this is merged and deployed.

## TEST 1: Share link

1. Open a published invoice on the live site.
2. **Expect:** "Share with client" shows `https://satsendofficial.vercel.app/invoice/…`
   (or `https://satsend.me/invoice/…` once the domain is connected), not
   `http://localhost:3000/…`.

## TEST 2: Email link

1. On the live site, publish a test invoice with "Send now via email" to your
   own address.
2. **Expect:** "View and pay" and the written-out link both open the live invoice
   page, from your phone as well as your computer.

## TEST 3: PDF link

1. Download that invoice's PDF.
2. **Expect:** the link printed on it is the live site address, not localhost.

## TEST 4: Local dev unchanged

1. Locally, open an invoice. **Expect:** the share link is still
   `http://localhost:3000/invoice/…`.
