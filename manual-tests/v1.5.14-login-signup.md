# v1.5.14 — Separate "Log in" and "Create account": manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.14/login-signup` · **Package version:** `1.5.14` · **Migration:** `0033`

Run locally (`npm run dev`, test database SatSend-dev, testnet) unless a test says
production. TESTS 4–6 need a real inbox you can read; for TEST 4 use a brand-new
address (Gmail's `you+satsend2@gmail.com` works).

## Quick smoke test

If you only have a couple of minutes, run **TEST 1**, **TEST 3** and **TEST 4**.

## TEST 1: Log in with an email that has no account

**Proves:** logging in never creates an account.

1. **Run:** open `/login`, enter an address that has never used SatSend, press
   **Send sign-in link**.
2. **Expect:** "There's no SatSend account for that email." with a **Create an
   account** button. No "Check your email" screen, and no email arrives.
3. **DB confirmation:** Supabase (SatSend-dev) → Authentication → Users: no user
   with that address.

## TEST 2: Switching pages keeps the email

**Proves:** you never have to retype it.

1. **Run:** from TEST 1, press **Create an account**. Then press "Already have
   an account? **Log in**".
2. **Expect:** `/signup` and then `/login` open with the same email already filled in.

## TEST 3: Create account with an email that already has an account

**Proves:** nobody signs up twice by mistake, and nothing is sent.

1. **Run:** on `/signup`, enter your existing address, press **Create account**.
2. **Expect:** "You already have an account with that email." with **Log in
   instead**, which opens `/login` with the email filled in. No email arrives.

## TEST 4: Create account with a new email

**Proves:** the sign-up happy path still works.

1. **Run:** on `/signup`, enter a brand-new address, press **Create account**.
2. **Expect:** "Check your email · We've sent a link to confirm your account to
   …". The "Confirm your SatSend account" email arrives; its button signs you in
   and lands on `/dashboard`.

## TEST 5: Log in with an existing email

**Proves:** the log-in happy path still works.

1. **Run:** sign out, then on `/login` enter your address, press **Send sign-in link**.
2. **Expect:** "We've sent a sign-in link to …". The "Your SatSend sign-in
   link" email arrives; its button lands on `/dashboard`.

## TEST 6: Used link, and asking twice

**Proves:** the two page fixes.

1. **Run:** click the button in TEST 5's email a second time (private window).
   Then on `/login` ask for two links within a few seconds.
2. **Expect:** the first lands on `/login` with "That link has expired or was
   already used. Enter your email and we'll send a new one." The second shows
   "Please wait a minute before asking for another link." and stays on the form.

## TEST 7: Sign-up limit (production, after merge)

**Proves:** the firewall rule stops the sign-up check being used to test lists of addresses.

1. **Run:** six times in a row:
   `for i in 1 2 3 4 5 6; do curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" -X POST https://satsend.me/api/auth/signup-check -H "content-type: application/json" -d '{"email":"limit-test@example.com"}'; done`
2. **Expect:** `200` five times, then `429`. (This uses up your connection's
   allowance for an hour; it creates nothing and sends nothing.)
