# v1.5.37 — Actions that can't run are greyed out with the reason

**Branch:** `v1.5.37/row-action-availability` · **Package version:** `1.5.37` · **Migration:** none

## Setup (once)

1. Check out the branch: `git checkout v1.5.37/row-action-availability`
2. Start the dev server: `npm run dev`
3. Sign in at http://localhost:3000 with your own account (test database).

You need two invoices:

- **A draft with no bitcoin address and no client email.** New invoice, fill in
  a client name and one line item, leave the bitcoin address and client email
  empty, then **Save draft**.
- **A sent invoice** (any status except Draft). If you don't have one, make a
  draft with an address and use **Send → Copy link and mark as sent**.

## TEST 1 — Invoice list: a draft with no address can't be sent

**Proves:** the send options are greyed out, and you're told why, once.

**Run:** on the Invoices list, open the **⋯** menu on the draft with no address.

**Expect:**
- "Send now via email", "Download PDF and mark as sent" and "Copy link and mark
  as sent" are all greyed out.
- Under "Send now via email", a small grey line reads: *Add a bitcoin address
  to this draft before sending it.* It appears once, not under every item.
- Clicking a greyed-out item does nothing.
- Duplicate and Delete are still available.

## TEST 2 — Invoice page: the same draft

**Proves:** the invoice page's Send menu follows the same rule.

**Run:** open that draft, click **Send**.

**Expect:** the same three greyed-out items, with the same single reason line.

## TEST 3 — The "no client email" reason now shows

**Proves:** the old reason, which could never be seen before, is now visible.

**Run:** edit the draft, add a valid bitcoin address (leave client email
empty), save. Back on the list, open its **⋯** menu.

**Expect:**
- "Send now via email" is greyed out, with: *No client email on this invoice,
  so it can't be sent by email.*
- "Download PDF and mark as sent" and "Copy link and mark as sent" are
  available. ("Copy link" still shows its usual grey hint, *Share the link
  yourself*.)

## TEST 4 — Delete on a sent invoice

**Proves:** Delete no longer silently does nothing; it's greyed out with the
reason.

**Run:**
1. On the Invoices list, open the **⋯** menu on the sent invoice.
2. Open the sent invoice's page and hover over **Delete**.

**Expect:**
1. In the menu, Delete is greyed out with: *Only drafts can be deleted. You can
   archive a sent invoice instead.*
2. On the page, Delete is greyed out, and hovering shows the same sentence.
   Clicking it does nothing (no dialog, you stay on the page).

## TEST 5 — Edit page: nothing greyed out, errors under the fields

**Proves:** on the form, the Send menu behaves the same way for every option:
nothing is greyed out, and a missing field is flagged under the field itself.

**Run:** open the draft from TEST 1 and click **Edit**. Clear the bitcoin
address and the client email. Click **Send**.

**Expect:**
1. All three options are clickable (none greyed out, no grey reason lines).
2. Click **Send now via email**: nothing is sent; red errors appear under the
   bitcoin address (*BTC address is required to send*) and under the client
   email (*Client email is required to send by email*).
3. Fill in a valid bitcoin address only, then click **Send → Download PDF and
   mark as sent** in a second check if you like: it goes through (no email is
   needed to send by hand). Skip this step if you want to keep the draft.
