# v1.5.12 — Email logo embedded in the email: manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.12/email-logo-inline` · **Package version:** `1.5.12`

## TEST 1: The logo shows in Proton Mail

**Proves:** an email app that wouldn't show the linked logo shows the embedded one.

1. **Run:** send an invoice email to a Proton Mail address (publish an invoice
   with "Send via email", or the one-off test script used on the branch).
2. **Expect:** the SatSend logo shows at the top, with no "load remote content"
   needed for it. No separate attachment appears in the attachment list (Proton
   may list it; that is acceptable).

## TEST 2: The logo still shows in Gmail

**Proves:** the change doesn't break the most common inbox.

1. **Run:** send an invoice email to a Gmail address.
2. **Expect:** the logo shows at the top; Gmail does not show a paperclip
   attachment for it.

## TEST 3: Sign-in emails unchanged

**Proves:** the Supabase emails still load the logo from the website.

1. **Run:** sign out and request a sign-in link on `satsend.me`.
2. **Expect:** the logo shows as before (in Gmail).
