# v1.5.16 — Marketing homepage: manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.16/marketing` · **Package version:** `1.5.16` · **Migration:** none

What the page should say is in `development/plans/v1.5.16-homepage-content-plan.md`.
No accounts are created and no email is sent by these tests.

## Quick smoke test

Run **TEST 1**, **TEST 2** and **TEST 4**.

## TEST 1: Signed out, `/` is the homepage

**Proves:** visitors land on the homepage, and its buttons go to the right places.

1. **Setup:** a private browser window (signed out). `npm run dev`.
2. **Run:** open `http://localhost:3000/`.
3. **Expect:**
   - Headline "Invoice in bitcoin. Paid straight to your wallet."
   - Top right: **Log in** and **Get started**.
   - **Get started** opens Create account (`/signup`).
   - **How it works** scrolls down to the three steps.
   - Pricing shows Free $0 and Pro $10 with a "Coming soon" tag.
   - The eight FAQ questions open and close.
4. **Status check:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" http://localhost:3000/`
   prints `200`.

## TEST 2: Signed in, `/` stays the homepage

**Proves:** signed-in users aren't bounced to the dashboard, and get a way into the app.

1. **Setup:** log in normally (you land on the dashboard).
2. **Run:** go to `http://localhost:3000/`.
3. **Expect:** the homepage (no jump to the dashboard). Top right shows only
   **Go to app**, which opens the dashboard.

## TEST 3: Every logo goes home

**Proves:** the "logo links home" rule lands on the new homepage.

1. **Run:** click the SatSend logo on: the dashboard, an invoice, a client
   invoice page (`/invoice/<id>`), Log in, Create account, and a placeholder page
   such as `/pricing`.
2. **Expect:** every click lands on the homepage.

## TEST 4: Phone width

**Proves:** the page works on a phone.

1. **Run:** browser dev tools → device toolbar → 390 × 844 (or a real phone).
2. **Expect:** no sideways scrolling anywhere; buttons easy to tap; the pricing
   cards stack; the footer shows two columns.

## TEST 5: No dead links

**Proves:** every menu, footer and social link opens a page.

1. **Run:** with the dev server running:
   `for p in / /how-it-works /pricing /faq /security /about /contact /privacy /terms /developers/api /coming-soon; do printf "%-16s " $p; curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" http://localhost:3000$p; done`
2. **Expect:** `200` on every line. Opening any of them (except `/`) shows the
   page name and "Coming soon", with a **Back to the homepage** button.

## TEST 6: The style guide is gone

**Proves:** the internal kit no longer ships.

1. **Run:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" http://localhost:3000/styleguide`
2. **Expect:** `404`.

## TEST 7: Link preview (production, after merge)

**Proves:** sharing satsend.me shows a proper preview.

1. **Run:** paste `https://satsend.me` into a link-preview checker (for example
   opengraph.xyz) or a chat app.
2. **Expect:** title "SatSend: Bitcoin invoicing, paid straight to your wallet",
   the description, and the image (amber "S" tile, "SatSend", "Bitcoin invoicing").
   **Status check:** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" https://satsend.me/opengraph-image` prints `200`.
