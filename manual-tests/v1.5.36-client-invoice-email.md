## TEST 1: The invoice email reads well and looks right

**Proves:** a client understands what this is and how to pay before clicking.

1. **Run:** open both **[Sample]** emails.
2. **Expect:**
   - the subject says who sent it and how much;
   - the heading is **Invoice INV-0043**;
   - a **How to pay** section with three short steps, then a grey line about
     the rate and that the payment goes straight to Maya Chen;
   - in the "with an access code" one, step 1 says to enter the access code,
     and the code is in the box above;
   - nothing looks crowded or out of place, on your phone too if you can.
