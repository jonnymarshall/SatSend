# v1.5.7 — Wider email column: manual tests

**Branch:** `v1.5.7/email-layout` · **Package version:** `1.5.7`

## TEST 1: Payment detected email on desktop

1. Trigger a payment-detected (or payment-confirmed) email, ideally from the live
   site after merging, and open it in Gmail on a computer.
2. **Expect:** the white card is noticeably wider than before (up to 720px), and
   the "Txid: …" line fits on one line.

## TEST 2: Same email on a phone

1. Open the same email in the Gmail app on a phone.
2. **Expect:** the card fills the screen with a small gap at the sides. The txid
   wraps onto two lines (it is too long for any phone screen); everything else
   reads normally.
