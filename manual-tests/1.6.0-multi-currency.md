# Release 1.6.0 (R-44) — Multi-currency

**Branch:** `r-44/multi-currency` · **Package version:** `1.6.0` · **Migration:** `0042_multi_currency.sql` (already applied to the test database)

Invoices can be in 33 currencies. The "close enough counts as paid" cap is still
$25, converted into the invoice's currency when the payment is checked.

## Setup (once)

1. `git checkout r-44/multi-currency`
2. `npm run dev`
3. Sign in at http://localhost:3000 with your own account (test database).

## Quick smoke test

TEST 1, TEST 2 and TEST 3.

## TEST 1 — An invoice in euros, everywhere it shows

**Proves:** no "$" anywhere on a euro invoice.

**Run:**
1. **New Invoice.** Next to **Invoice number**, the **Currency** box. Type `euro` and pick **EUR — Euro**.
2. Add a line item: quantity `2`, price `150`. Add a fresh testnet address and a client email.
3. Send it with **Send now via email**.
4. Look at: the form's totals before sending, your invoice page, the Invoices list, the client's email, the client's page (open the link), and **Download PDF**.

**Expect:**
- The form shows Total **€300.00**.
- Every place shows **€150.00**, **€300.00** and so on, never "$".

## TEST 2 — The bitcoin amount uses the euro price

**Run:** on the client's page from TEST 1, click **Pay now in Bitcoin**.

**Expect:**
- The price line under the amount says **at €…/BTC**, not "$".
- The BTC amount is roughly €300 at today's euro price. Check against any BTC/EUR price: €300 ÷ price ≈ the amount shown.

## TEST 3 — A real testnet payment in pounds

**Proves:** payments are judged and shown in the invoice's currency.

**Run:**
1. New invoice in **GBP — British Pound** for £50 with a fresh testnet address. Send it.
2. On the client's page, **Pay now in Bitcoin**, and pay the exact amount from your testnet wallet.
3. Wait for it to confirm (about 20 to 40 minutes on testnet).

**Expect:**
- It goes Payment detected, then **Paid**.
- The payment breakdown on the client's page, your page, the PDF and the "paid" emails is in pounds (£50.00 requested, £… received).

## TEST 4 — Changing a draft's currency

**Run:**
1. Open a draft in USD, click **Edit draft**, change the currency to **CAD — Canadian Dollar**, save.
2. Open a sent invoice.

**Expect:**
1. The invoice page shows **CA$** amounts.
2. A sent invoice has no Edit, so its currency can't change.

## TEST 5 — The next invoice starts in your last currency

**Run:** after TEST 4, click **New Invoice**.

**Expect:** the Currency box already says your most recent invoice's currency (e.g. CAD). Change it back to USD if you like.

## TEST 6 — Duplicate keeps the currency

**Run:** on the TEST 1 euro invoice, **More → Duplicate**.

**Expect:** the new form's Currency is **EUR — Euro**.

## TEST 7 — Old invoices

**Run:** open an invoice made before this release.

**Expect:** still in dollars, as before.

## TEST 8 — Phone width

**Run:** **New Invoice** in a narrow window (or the browser's phone view).

**Expect:** the Currency box sits under Invoice number, full width, and its list opens and scrolls.
