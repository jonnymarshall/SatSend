## Decisions (taken 2026-10-09)

1. **Status text:** AA shades. *(your call)*
2. **Links and the hero's amber line:** links are **ink text with an amber
   underline**; the hero line uses a slightly deeper amber, `#BC8925`, the lightest
   one that is readable at that size. *(recommended, adopted; see TEST 7)*
3. **Input outline and focus border:** outline `#949495`, focus border `#BC8925`,
   both the minimum needed to be clearly visible. *(recommended, adopted)*
4. **Pending vs Payment detected:** Pending stays blue, label "Pending"; Payment
   detected moves to **violet** `#8B5CF6`, as the brand brief intended. *(from your
   note that the two looked almost the same)*

Anything here can be flipped; say which and it is a one-line change.

### TEST 7 — Contrast check

Contrast is how much a colour stands out from what is behind it. Small text needs
4.5:1; big headings and control outlines need 3:1.

- **Run:** section 8 ("Contrast check").
- **Expect:** the summary says **"Everything we use now passes (16 of 16)"**, and
  "The handoff had 12 that did not." Every row's "Now" column says Pass.
