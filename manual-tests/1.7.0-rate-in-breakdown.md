# Release 1.7.0 (R-45) — Exchange rate in the payment breakdown

**Branch:** `r-45/rate-in-breakdown` · **Package version:** `1.7.0` · **Migration:** none

Once a payment is judged, the bitcoin price it was valued at shows as
"Exchange rate: 1 BTC = …", in the invoice's currency: on the client's page,
in the payment emails, on the PDF, and on your own invoice page.

## Setup (once)

1. `git checkout r-45/rate-in-breakdown`
2. `npm run dev`
3. Sign in at http://localhost:3000 with your own account (test database).

## Quick smoke test

TEST 1 and TEST 2.

## TEST 1 — Your own invoice page

**Proves:** you can see the rate without opening the public page.

**Run:** open your paid £50 invoice from 1.6.0's TEST 3.

**Expect:**
- A **Payment** panel under the header with **Received** (£… and the BTC amount) and **Exchange rate** (1 BTC = £…).
- Received BTC × the rate ≈ the pounds received.

## TEST 2 — The client's page and the PDF

**Run:**
1. On the same invoice, **View public invoice**.
2. Then **Download PDF**.

**Expect:** both show **Exchange rate 1 BTC = £…** under **Paid on**, the same figure as TEST 1.

## TEST 3 — The "Paid" email

**Run:** a new small invoice with a fresh testnet address and your email as the client's. Pay it from your testnet wallet and wait for it to confirm (about 20 minutes).

**Expect:**
- The "Payment detected" email has no exchange rate (the payment isn't valued yet).
- The "Paid" emails (yours and the client's) show **Exchange rate: 1 BTC = …**.

## TEST 4 — An invoice marked paid by hand

**Run:** open an invoice you marked as paid yourself (no bitcoin payment).

**Expect:** no Payment panel and no exchange rate anywhere.

## TEST 5 — Phone width

**Run:** TEST 1 and the client's page in a narrow window (or the browser's phone view).

**Expect:** the rate fits on one line beside its label.
