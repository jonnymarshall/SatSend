# v1.5.3 — Email deliverability (code side): manual tests

**Branch:** `fix/email-delivery` · **Package version:** `1.5.3`

## TEST 1: Plain-text version and visible link

1. Publish a test invoice with "Send now via email" to your own address.
2. Open the email (check spam too). In Gmail: ⋮ → "Show original".
3. **Expect:** the original has two parts, `text/plain` and `text/html`.
4. **Expect:** under the "View and pay" button: "Or open this link:" followed by
   the invoice address written out.

## TEST 2: Replies go to you

1. Reply to that invoice email.
2. **Expect:** the reply is addressed to your own SatSend account email, not to
   the SatSend sending address.

## TEST 3: Spam check (after the domain fixes)

Only meaningful once the DMARC record is fixed and the app runs on `satsend.me`
(see `development/OUTSTANDING-VERIFICATIONS.md`).

1. In "Show original", **expect** `SPF: PASS`, `DKIM: PASS`, `DMARC: PASS`.
2. **Expect:** the email arrives in the inbox. If it still lands in spam, mark it
   "Not spam" once; new domains build reputation slowly.
